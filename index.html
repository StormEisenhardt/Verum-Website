A whole team, not just a chat box

Verum turns the best AI engines into colleagues with names, jobs and memory — and keeps you in charge.

Named, lasting agents

Make agents with a name, a role and a job description. They remember the work, keep their own folder and pick up where they left off.

Three engines

Each agent runs on Claude Code, Codex or Hermes, using your own Claude, ChatGPT or Nous Portal subscription.

You approve the risky parts

Before an agent spends money, sends something or deletes things, it stops and asks you. One tap to approve or deny.

Learns what you like

Verum remembers your preferences and rules over time, and every agent gets the same briefing about you.

Teams and projects

Put agents in a project with a lead. They hand each other tasks and files, and you see the whole org chart.

Schedules

Daily reports, weekly clean-ups, a reminder in an hour: agents run tasks on a schedule while you are away.

Private by design

Your agents live on your box

Verum runs on a computer you own — a Raspberry Pi, a Linux machine or your Windows PC. Chats, files and memories stay there. Your Verum account only knows who you are, which boxes are yours and how much they are used.

  • Only the account owner can sign in to the box
  • Works on your phone through your own private network
  • Sign out everywhere with one click if you lose a device
